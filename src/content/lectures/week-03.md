Light is not just something a shot needs enough of — its direction, quality and colour temperature are authorial decisions that change what a viewer feels and where they look. This lecture compares front, side and back light, breaks down hard versus soft light and Kelvin colour temperature, and connects both to the three-shot lighting exercise and Assignment 1's light study.

## Outline

We work through the three-variable framework — direction, quality, colour — with front/side/back light comparisons, three-point lighting ratios, and the golden-hour-to-blue-hour colour temperature range. The session closes with the three-shot exercise: use changing light alone to alter the meaning of a routine, then plan the location, time of day and backup light for Assignment 1's light study.

Bring two examples of the same place photographed or filmed at different times of day.
