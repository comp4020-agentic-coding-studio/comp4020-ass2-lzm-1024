This is the last checkpoint before you lock picture and sound. A final peer review looks for finishing errors, not structural ones — caption drift, missing credits, continuity slips and audio levels — while a separate pass audits consent and external-media records against what the finished film actually shows and where it will actually be seen.

Lock only after that checklist is clear and you have watched the actual export on the actual device it will be judged on. The lecture also works through what makes the final project's 400-word reflection substantive rather than descriptive, and reframes the submission dossier as an argument for the choices you made, not a compliance list.
