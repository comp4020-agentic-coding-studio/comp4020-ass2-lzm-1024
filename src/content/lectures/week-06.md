A logline only becomes a shoot once it is broken into a structure, a
storyboard and a shot list that someone else could execute. This lecture
maps setup, development and resolution as images, builds a shot list from a
storyboard, and works through coverage, screen direction and continuity
before turning to the planning a real shoot day needs: time budgeting, risk
assessment, access and permission, and a backup plan.

## Outline

We move from a logline and a rough list of moments to a three-part structure,
then from storyboard panels to a full shot list with size, movement,
location and duration columns. The session closes with the planning work a
shoot depends on — risk, permission, consent and the media record, and a
backup for anything the plan assumes — and with the production log you keep
once a shoot changes on the day.

Bring a logline, a location and a rough list of moments, and be ready to
trade shot lists with a partner for review before Week 7.
