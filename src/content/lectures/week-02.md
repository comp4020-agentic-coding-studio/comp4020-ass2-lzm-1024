Technical settings are creative choices because they affect what a viewer can see and hear. We will use a phone and a camera to compare automatic and deliberate exposure, focus and movement.

## Outline

Students will make a 20-second continuity exercise, review its shot list, then prepare a production plan for Assignment 1. The aim is reliable footage, not expensive equipment.
