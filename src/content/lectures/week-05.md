A documentary subject's truth can arrive two ways: spoken back to you in an interview, or shown to you through unprompted behaviour. This lecture compares interview-led and observational production, works through interview technique — open questions, sequencing, silence, off-camera eyeline — and the ethics of cutaways, B-roll and trimming an answer without changing what someone meant.

## Outline

We trace interview-led and observational method back to their cinéma vérité and direct cinema roots, then build the practical skills each needs: consent as an ongoing conversation rather than a one-time form, open versus closed questions, off-camera interviewer positioning, and the two jobs a cutaway can do — covering a jump cut or backing up a claim — and the false correspondence risk when those two jobs blur. The session closes with a role-play review of when each mode serves a story better, and confirming your Assignment 1 subject and their permission.

Bring a rough idea of who you want to film for Assignment 1, and be ready to have the consent conversation with a partner in session.
