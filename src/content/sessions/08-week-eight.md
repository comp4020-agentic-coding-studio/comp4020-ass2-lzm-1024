## Before the session

Bring one sentence describing your final project subject and a reference video whose rhythm you want to study.

## In the session

We diagnose continuity problems, re-cut a shared sequence and workshop final-project premises for scope and access.

## Afterwards

Write a final-project logline, audience statement and first production plan.

## Further practice

- [StudioBinder: the 180-degree rule](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/180-degree-rule-film/) gives a visual explanation of screen direction and when a deliberate line crossing can work.
- [Adobe: create a project and import media](https://www.adobe.com/learn/premiere-pro/web/create-project-import-media) supports a clean edit workflow before you begin recutting coverage.

## Reference viewing

Choose a scene with a clear action. Track screen direction: when does the editor preserve orientation, and when do they disrupt it?
