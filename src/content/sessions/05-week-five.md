## Before the session

Bring a draft consent conversation and three open questions, or an observational plan that does not require an interview.

## In the session

We practise introductions, permissions and cutaway shots. A role-play review identifies when an interview provides useful evidence and when observation says more.

## Afterwards

Confirm your Assignment 1 subject and seek permission before your main shoot.

## Further practice

- [Center for Media & Social Impact: Documentary Ethics](https://cmsimpact.org/program/documentary-ethics/) provides practical prompts for consent, representation and the responsibilities of filmmakers.
- [National Film and Sound Archive: collection and learning](https://www.nfsa.gov.au/collection/curated/learn) is a useful place to study how Australian documentaries frame people and place.

## Reference viewing

Observe an interview-led short work. Which questions remain unheard, and how do cutaway shots affect your trust in the speaker?
