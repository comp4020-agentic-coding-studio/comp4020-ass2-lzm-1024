## Before the session

Bring three Week 1 shots and your note about the change they show. Check that your files play from the device you bring.

## In the session

Small groups identify what each sequence communicates. We then test focus, exposure lock, stabilisation and frame rate using a 20-second continuity exercise.

## Afterwards

Draft a one-sentence premise for [Assignment 1](/assessments/assignment-1/). Choose a subject you can approach safely and repeatedly.

## Technical preparation

For the Week 2 laboratory exercise, record two ten-second tests of the same subject: one in daylight and one indoors. Label each clip with its frame rate, shutter, ISO, white-balance setting and focus mode. Bring both the clips and the settings record to class.

## Further practice

- [Adobe: Video encoding settings reference](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/render-and-export/export-files/video-encoding-settings.html) explains the relationship between format, resolution, frame rate and codec at export.
- [Blackmagic Design: DaVinci Resolve training](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/au/products/davinciresolve/training) provides free official editing, colour and audio practice materials.
- [W3C: Making Audio and Video Media Accessible](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/) explains captions, transcripts and audio description as part of a responsible video workflow.
