## Before the session

Bring headphones if you have them and collect ten seconds of ambient sound from a place you may use in Assignment 1.

## In the session

We test distance, room tone, wind and monitoring. Students record a short sound portrait and compare what the image alone cannot tell us.

## Afterwards

Revise your production plan to include sound: what will the viewer hear at the beginning, middle and end?

## Further practice

- [W3C: Making Audio and Video Media Accessible](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/) explains how meaningful speech and non-speech sound should be represented through captions and transcripts.
- [Blackmagic Design: Fairlight Audio Guide](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/au/products/davinciresolve/training) provides an official starting point for recording, editing and mixing sound in DaVinci Resolve.

## Reference viewing

Watch a scene once with your eyes closed. What information does sound provide that the image does not?
