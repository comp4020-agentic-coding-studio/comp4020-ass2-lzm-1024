## Before the session

Bring headphones if you have them and collect ten seconds of ambient sound from a place you may use in Assignment 1.

## In the session

We test distance, room tone, wind and monitoring. Students record a short sound portrait and compare what the image alone cannot tell us.

## Afterwards

Revise your production plan to include sound: what will the viewer hear at the beginning, middle and end?
