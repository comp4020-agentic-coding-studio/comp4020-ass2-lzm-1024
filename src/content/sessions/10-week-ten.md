## Before the session

Bring a 90-second to 3-minute rough cut, a short list of questions for viewers and your media record.

## In the session

Small groups watch work once without interruption, then give feedback anchored to observable choices. We separate a useful revision from a request to make every project look alike.

## Afterwards

Choose two feedback changes and document why you will make them.

## Further practice

- [University of North Carolina Writing Center: film analysis](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/film-analysis/) models how to connect a specific formal choice with its effect rather than offering vague praise.
- [Adobe Learn: Premiere tutorials](https://www.adobe.com/learn/premiere-pro) lets you target a revision problem, such as trimming, sound cleanup or colour correction, with a focused exercise.

## Reference viewing

Read comments on a short work critically. Which feedback identifies an observable choice, and which merely states a preference?
