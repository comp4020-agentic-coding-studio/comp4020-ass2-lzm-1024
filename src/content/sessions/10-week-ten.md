## Before the session

Bring a 90-second to 3-minute rough cut, a short list of questions for viewers and your media record.

## In the session

Small groups watch work once without interruption, then give feedback anchored to observable choices. We separate a useful revision from a request to make every project look alike.

## Afterwards

Choose two feedback changes and document why you will make them.
