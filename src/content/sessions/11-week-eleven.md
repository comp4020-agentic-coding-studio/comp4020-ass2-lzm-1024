## Before the session

Bring a revised final-project cut, captions and a draft dossier.

## In the session

We run a final peer review, check captions and credits, and audit consent and external media records.

## Afterwards

Lock your picture and sound only after completing a final viewing on the device you will use to export.

## Further practice

- [Creative Commons: license and attribution guidance](https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/cclicenses/) helps you identify which media can be reused and how to credit it accurately.
- [W3C: accessible media guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/) supports a final captions, transcript and audio-description check before submission.

## Reference viewing

Watch a final film twice. On the second viewing, identify one revision you think improved the audience’s understanding.
