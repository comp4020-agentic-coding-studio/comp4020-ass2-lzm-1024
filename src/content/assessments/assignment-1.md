## The portfolio

Build a small portfolio from the first six weeks of studio work. It should show a progression from individual technical decisions to an edited visual story.

## Download the assignment brief

[Download the Assignment 1 requirements PDF](/assessment-briefs/SLOP1416-assignment-1-brief.pdf)

1. **Framing a change:** a three-shot sequence using wide, medium and close shots.
2. **Light study:** the same subject recorded in two contrasting lighting conditions, with a 100-word comparison.
3. **Sound portrait:** a 30-second sequence led by ambient sound or clear voice.
4. **A small change:** a 60–90 second non-fiction video with a recognisable beginning and ending.

The final sequence needs purposeful sound and captions. You may work with one collaborator, but each student submits an individual reflection.

## What you submit

Upload a folder containing each exercise as an H.264 MP4, 1080p or lower. Add one PDF containing a contents list, the light-study comparison, a logline and shot list for the final sequence, a 250-word reflection and a media/consent record. Name files with your student number and `a1`.

## Feedback

You will receive rubric feedback in the course site. Use it to choose one craft priority for the final project.
